# Executive report content and diagnostic evidence

Status: accepted for implementation, 2026-09-30.

The two existing Word actions produce the principal report and supplement.
The saved reference defines business section order, quarter and budget coverage,
and remaining-position detail. Historical arithmetic errors and unsupported
contract/execution claims are not reproduced.

ReportModel carries those complete prepared sections. Renderers format them
without reading live sources or calculating business metrics. An independent
raw-cell audit checks section amounts and populations before publication.

Engine identifiers, source columns, candidate lists and validation codes belong
to a saved diagnostic protocol. DOCX core properties bind an artifact to its
snapshot; visible content retains its business date. HTTP acceptance verifies
both. Existing saved releases remain immutable.

Recommendation origin, current identity and fulfillment are separate results.
Only evidenced business findings enter the executive table; concrete unresolved
business questions remain visible without implying noncompliance. Original
responses and decisions are preserved. Technical evidence remains in the bundle.
